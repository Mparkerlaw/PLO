# Client Intake Procedure

## Initial Contact

1. Record client name, phone, email, and referral source
2. Brief description of legal issue
3. Check for conflicts of interest
4. Schedule consultation (in-person or virtual)

## Consultation

1. Review facts and documents provided by client
2. Identify legal issues and potential claims/defenses
3. Discuss fee structure and engagement terms
4. If retained — proceed to engagement steps below

## Engagement

1. Send engagement letter / fee agreement for signature
2. Collect retainer (if applicable)
3. Open matter in case management system
4. Create client folder (physical and/or digital)
5. Send welcome packet with next steps
6. Calendar key dates and deadlines
